ACTIVITY GUIDE & RULES

Run your way. Submit it clearly.

Use the fitness app you already have. Follow these simple rules so your activity can be verified quickly.

1Run or walk outdoors
2Record with your app
3Submit link or screenshot
4Approved KM joins the challenge

✓ What counts

Outdoor run or walk recorded by a fitness/activity app.

Completed from 15 October to 16 November 2026.

Proof clearly shows date, distance and duration or pace.

The activity is completed by the participant submitting it.

× What doesn’t count

Treadmill or indoor running, cycling or other sports.

Manually created activity without valid tracking proof.

Activities outside the campaign period.

Duplicate/reused proof or proof that cannot be verified.
